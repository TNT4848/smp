Rules

  1. No mods or resource packs that provide an unfair advantage unless permitted by the owner. Use common sense. If unsure, ask. Examples include X-Ray, Fly hacks, Autocrit, KillAura, Aimbot, and Player tracking.
  2. No griefing. If damage takes the owner over 5 minutes to fix, it is considered griefing. If enough people (including the owner) agree a build is ugly, it may be demolished. The owner cannot intentionally take over 5 minutes to fix obvious non-griefing damage.
  3. No duplication glitches, excluding lit TNT duping. This includes gravity block duping, tripwire hook duping, carpet duping, or any other dupe glitch.
  4. Be respectful. Do not use derogatory words or slurs in Minecraft or Discord chats.
  5. No destructive devices such as lag machines, chunk bans, or similar exploits.

Recording is advised so if you catch someone breaking the rules, you can easily report them.
